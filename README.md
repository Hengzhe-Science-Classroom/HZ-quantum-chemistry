# Quantum Chemistry · Interactive

**Electronic structure calculations and molecular modeling**

**Status:** Planned

Part of [Hengzhe's Science Classroom](https://hengzhe-science-classroom.github.io/hub/) · Tier 5 · Research Frontier

## What You'll Learn

- Hydrogen atom and atomic orbitals
- Hartree-Fock theory
- Electron correlation: CI, MP2, CC
- Density functional theory (DFT)
- Basis sets and practical calculations
- Molecular properties and spectroscopy

## References

- Attila Szabo & Neil S. Ostlund, *Modern Quantum Chemistry* (Dover)
- Ira N. Levine, *Quantum Chemistry* (7th ed., Pearson)
- Frank Jensen, *Introduction to Computational Chemistry* (3rd ed., Wiley)

## Features (when built)

- **Interactive visualizations** — every concept comes alive with draggable, animated demos
- **KaTeX math rendering** — rigorous notation throughout
- **Exercises with hints & solutions** — from computational to proof-based
- **Progress tracking** — saved locally in your browser
- **Zero dependencies** — just open the link and learn
